# Market map

Running file. Last updated 2026-11-11 (night 1).

Figures are as reported by the linked source. Anything marked "unverified" came from a search summary and the page was not opened. Nothing here is my own estimate unless labelled.

## Niche 1: AI-proof human businesses (connection, presence, purpose)

**Who pays whom.** Consumers pay subscriptions to apps (meditation, stranger dinners). Organisers pay platforms a monthly fee to list groups. Retreat leaders pay booking software a subscription plus payment fees. Venues get paid per head.

| Player | What it sells | Price | Money and growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calm | Meditation app | subscription | About $300M revenue in 2023, down 15% (estimate, [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/?p=60332)) |
| Headspace | Meditation app | subscription | About $195M in 2023, down from about $235M in 2022; paid subscribers fell to 2.8M (estimate, [Business of Apps](https://businessofapps.com/?p=60411)) |
| Timeleft | Dinners with strangers | about $20/month in the US (blog-reported, unverified) | Funding not found |
| 222 | Matched in-person outings | not found | $10.1M Series A led by Upfront Ventures, December 2025 ([Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/companies/222/funding-rounds/series-a-2025-12)) |
| Pie | Group events, groups of six | not found | $11.5M Series A, about $24M total; reported to be shifting from one-off events to permanent communities in 2026 ([report](https://ecosistemastartup.com/?p=101464), unverified) |
| Meetup | Group listings | organiser fee reported up from $24 to $45/month (unverified, [summary](https://nexspark.org/journal/meetup-alternatives)) | Owned by Bending Spoons (same source) |
| Heylo | Tools for lasting groups | Free, $19, $59, $199/month ([pricing](https://heylo.com/pricing)) | Not found |
| WeTravel | Group trip and retreat payments | from $79/month ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/149144/Retreat-Booking-Guru/)) | Not found |
| Retreat Guru | Retreat centre booking | listed tiers $67 to $422 ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/149144/Retreat-Booking-Guru/pricing/)) | Not found |

**New in the last 12 months.** 222's Series A (December 2025). Wygo, Toronto, nearly C$1.6M pre-seed and 10,000+ tickets sold by April 2026 ([BetaKit](https://betakit.com/?p=403027)). Retreat tooling entrants seen in search: SquadTrip, Gomry, Flowdara.

**Growing.** In-person matching and "loneliness" startups. **Dying.** Long-form meditation apps: demand for Calm and Headspace has fallen since 2020 ([Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20231215/amp?f=4)).

**Pay a lot and still complain.**
- Meetup organisers (fee rise, features moved behind a paywall; unverified).
- Timeleft subscribers (billing after cancellation, each dinner a new set of strangers; Trustpilot via search summary, not opened).

## Niche 2: Finance and investing tools

**Who pays whom.** Retail investors pay trackers a subscription; brokers earn on spreads, interest and cash; budgeting tools pay bank-data aggregators.

| Player | What it sells | Price (review sites, conflicting) |
|---|---|---|
| Sharesight | Portfolio and tax reporting | free to about AUD$50/month |
| Snowball Analytics | Dividend-focused tracker | about $9.99/month |
| Kubera | Net-worth tracker | no free plan reported |
| Empower | Net worth and portfolio | free |
| getquin | Tracker with social feed | free, Pro about EUR 4.99/month |

Source: [AllInvestView comparison](https://www.allinvestview.com/articles/best-portfolio-tracker/) and [freenance review](https://freenance.io/products/sharesight-review-2026-portfolio-tracker-dividend-tracking-tax/); both sites sell or promote competing tools. No funding data found.

**Seen first-hand in forums this month.**
- Trading 212: anger at a new buy/sell screen (54 replies) and new cashback terms (53 replies).
- Tiller: bank feed and sheet-linking failures; Tiller is shipping an MCP server and console categories.
- Portfolio123: buyers of paid "Designer Models" asking for statistical vetting.

**Pay and still complain.** Tiller users on bank-feed reliability. A fix needs aggregator contracts, so it is not a solo build.

## Niche 3: Developer and AI tooling

**Who pays whom.** Developers and companies pay coding-agent vendors per seat plus usage; vendors pay model providers. Agencies and freelancers get paid by small businesses to build n8n, Make, Bubble and Glide automations.

| Player | Reported |
|---|---|
| Cursor | Over $2B annualised revenue, March 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/03/02/cursor-has-reportedly-surpassed-2b-in-annualized-revenue/)); talks to raise $2B at about $50B ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/cursor-anysphere-2-billion-funding-50-billion-valuation-ai-coding)). One database lists it as acquired in August 2026; unverified. |
| GitHub Copilot | 29% workplace usage in one survey (unverified) |
| Claude Code | 18% workplace usage in the same survey (unverified) |
| Replit, Cognition, Lovable | Named as other funded players |
| Windsurf, Supermaven | Listed as acquired |

Funding is concentrated: one tracker puts median funding for AI coding startups at about $1M ([tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/ie/ai-coding-startups-shakeout-2026/), unverified).

**Seen first-hand in forums this month.**
- Cursor forum: usage-meter threads (194 and 65 replies), new subscription thread (370 posts).
- OpenAI community: "keep ChatGPT and Codex usage limits separate" (1,243 likes).
- Bubble: outage thread (193 posts), "disoriented by AI building" (22 posts).
- n8n and Make: hiring posts dominate; reliability and approval questions recur.

**Pay a lot and still complain.** Coding-agent subscribers on usage limits and opaque meters. Spend tracking is already crowded (TokenShift, Vantage, ForgeMeter, UsageAtlas per [PointFive](https://www.pointfive.co/blog/how-to-track-coding-agent-spend-across-your-team)).
